# Business rules — content policy

- BR-1 The docs describe only shipped behavior; roadmap items never appear as if
  they exist.
- BR-2 Documented commands and samples are re-verified against each release
  before its version is published.
- BR-3 No customer data, internal hostnames or credentials in pages or images.
- BR-4 Every release ships with release notes on the docs site on release day.
- BR-5 Outdated pages are corrected or removed, never left with a "may be
  outdated" disclaimer.
