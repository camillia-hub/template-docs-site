# Navigation conventions

- Top-level sections (the `nav:` of `mkdocs.yml`): Getting started, Tutorials,
  How-to guides, Reference, Explanation, Release notes. New sections need a
  docs-review discussion first.
- Section order in the nav matches the reader's journey: beginner to advanced.
- Page titles in the nav are short (four words or fewer); the page H1 may be
  longer.
- Related pages cross-link at the bottom under a "Next steps" heading.
- Release notes get one page per release (`release-notes/1.4.md`) plus an index
  entry; after the release ships they change only for errata marked as such.
